About me

Meet Khizar

Welcome to my portfolio. I’m Khizar, a Graphic Designer dedicated to turning complex ideas into visual masterpieces. From custom branding to polished video edits, I create visuals that make your brand stand out.

I also use AI tools to explore ideas faster and turn them into polished visuals.

What I do

Services

  • Logo and branding

    Logos and complete brand identities that stay sharp on every screen and in print.

  • Social media design

    Posts, stories and reel covers that make your whole feed look like one brand.

  • Posters, flyers and banners

    Print and digital pieces for events, offers and ads, delivered print-ready.

  • Photo editing

    Retouching, colour correction and clean cut-outs for portraits and products.

  • Video editing

    Polished edits for reels, shorts and promos, cut to keep people watching.

  • Cards and packaging

    Business cards, labels and boxes laid out for print, with mockups to preview them.

  • Thumbnails and mockups

    Thumbnails made to be clicked, and realistic mockups that show your design in the real world.

  • AI-assisted design

    AI tools used to explore ideas faster and create visuals, then refined into polished, on-brand work.
